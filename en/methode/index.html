Process 

A clear project from the first call to launch. At every step, you know what we do and what you get.

  1. 01

    30 minutes

    We listen

    A call to understand your business, your clients and what you want to achieve. We ask the questions, you have nothing to prepare.

    You getA written summary of the call

  2. 02

    Within 48 h

    We propose

    A detailed quote: what we do, what we don't, the price and the delivery date. The quoted price does not change along the way.

    You getThe quote and the timeline

  3. 03

    3 to 16 weeks

    We build

    We build and show you the progress every week. You follow the current step online, with your request number.

    You getA version to test every week

  4. 04

    After go-live

    We launch and support

    Go-live, training to manage your site, then one month of fixes included. After that, monthly support if you want it.

    You getAccess, training and one month of support
