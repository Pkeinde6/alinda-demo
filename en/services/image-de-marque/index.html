A brand identity in three weeks

Four steps, one meeting at each. You see the work move forward and you decide every time.

  1. 01Week 1

    Workshop

    Two hours to talk about your business, your clients and the brands you like. We leave with three directions in words.

  2. 02Week 1 to 2

    Directions

    Three designed directions: logo, colours and an example in context. You keep one.

  3. 03Week 2

    Refinement

    Two rounds of changes on the chosen direction, until the logo works small and large.

  4. 04Week 3

    Handover

    All the files, the brand guide and the templates. A session to show you how to use them.

What you get

  • Logo in SVG, PNG and PDF, in colour, black and white
  • A 12-page brand guide as a PDF
  • Canva templates for Instagram and Facebook
  • Business card and email signature, print-ready
  • Full rights to your logo
